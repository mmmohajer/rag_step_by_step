Fieldnote billing and cancellation

Fieldnote is a notes app for small teams. This page is the written billing policy. Account access, including passwords and locked sign-ins, is not covered here.

Plans

Studio is 12 dollars a month. Studio Annual is 120 dollars a year, billed once.

Invoices

An invoice is due 15 days after the invoice date. After the due date, the unpaid balance accrues 1.5 percent per month.

Cancellation and refunds

The workspace owner cancels from the billing page. A teammate cannot cancel the workspace.

An annual plan can be cancelled within 14 days of the charge for a full refund, and only if the workspace has invited no more than two teammates. After 14 days, an annual plan is not refunded.

A monthly plan stops at the end of the current period. A monthly plan is not refunded partway through the month.

A promotional price can be refunded only within 48 hours of the charge.
